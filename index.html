GPU-accelerated exact gradients make ODE ensembles tractable at scale now

GRADSOLVE: fast exact gradients for ODE ensembles on GPUs

Alessio Spurio Mancini · cs.MS · 9/10 signal

Integrate GRADSOLVE into your PyTorch ODE pipelines to halve gradient computation time.

Neural and scientific models increasingly rely on large ensembles of ordinary differential equations, but computing gradients through these solvers is a major bottleneck for training and inference.

GRADSOLVE rewrites the adjoint sensitivity method to run entirely on GPUs, delivering exact gradients for thousands of ODE instances in parallel without resorting to finite‑difference approximations.

Practitioners can drop it into existing PyTorch ODE code, halve gradient‑computation time, and scale ensembles that were previously infeasible, opening doors to richer physics‑informed models.
